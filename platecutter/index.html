Plate Cutter

Drop a plate here

A scan of a sequential photography plate (JPEG or PNG, or a page of a PDF). You mark each row, fix each frame's corners, and export transparent PNG sequences with the print's real edges.

Everything stays in your browser and is saved per plate.

Mark rows

Drag a box around the first frame of each row. The tool guesses the rest of the row. Drag again over a row's first frame to redo it.

    Scroll or two-finger drag to pan; pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom.

    Frame

    How far the cut may follow the print's real edge away from your corners.

    1 / 1

    The outgoing row's last frame, with the incoming row's first frame on top. Arrow keys move the incoming row and everything after it (Shift: 10px). In Difference, lined-up areas go dark.

    Preview & export

    Plays ping-pong or loop. Pause and click a sequence, then use the arrow keys to nudge the frame on screen (Shift for 10px).

    Sequences

    Each line plays its rows one after another. Drag a row to reorder it or move it to another line; a row can be used more than once.

    Rows

    Alignment, and which frames of the row the sequences use. With a sequence selected, [ and ] set the start and end of the row on screen at the current frame.

    One folder per sequence, frames numbered from 0001, plus a README with an After Effects expression that scrubs every angle together.
