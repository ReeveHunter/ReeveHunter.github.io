Fat NES

Play chords on one MIDI channel and get a big, gnarly instrument spread across all five NES channels, with DPCM intervals, noise layers, detune tricks and a pedal steel mode.

Lead idea

NES USB MIDI Synth

A MidiNES-style sound module for the Everdrive N8 Pro, fed from Logic through a Web MIDI + Web Serial page. Proper pitch bend, built-in vibrato, arpeggiator, echo, curated drum kits. The shared engine under Fat NES.

Music and synths

Sight Reading

Learn to sight-read piano music with a MIDI keyboard: adaptive note, interval, key, chord and melody practice, like a touch-typing course for the staff.

Web app · reevehunter.com/sightreading

Cynthcart Controller

Control Cynthcart on a Commodore 64 over MIDI: every sound parameter, saved patches, for Cynthcart 2.0 or the Kerberos custom build.

Web app · reevehunter.com/C64

Cynthcart Kerberos Build

A personal modification of Paul Slocum's Cynthcart synth for the C64 with a Kerberos MIDI cartridge: redesigned parameter display, per-voice editing, true tremolo, deeper vibrato and more. Not distributed, out of respect for Cynthcart's license.

Commodore 64 · private

Kerberos Flash

Flash PRGs and EasyFlash CRTs to the Kerberos C64/C128 MIDI cartridge from the browser, over Web MIDI.

Web app · reevehunter.com/flash

CZ-101 Patch Editor

A browser-based SysEx patch editor for the Casio CZ-101, CZ-1000 and CZ-5000.

Web app · reevehunter.com/CZ

gbmidi

A MIDI sound module for the original Game Boy, in the spirit of mGB. Every channel is played live from a DAW, and the wave channel adds a morphing wavetable synth, chord waves, a sampled drum kit and singable allophone speech. Name to be decided.

Stumped

A Game Boy–style Sokoban set in a forest at night, with 1,093 puzzles by David W. Skinner and a generative soundtrack your moves play along with. Plays in the browser and on the Miyoo Mini.

Browser and Miyoo Mini · reevehunter.com/stumped

Beanbag

A small audiobook and podcast player for the Miyoo Mini Plus running Onion OS. It remembers your place in every file, turns the screen off while you listen, and stays out of the way.
