Fat Chip NES

The NES member of Fat Chip: a MIDI sound module for the Everdrive N8 Pro, fed through a small bridge box that translates MIDI for the cart. Plays in tandem with Fat Chip 64 and Fat Chip GB, or alone from a DAW, with even pitch bends, built-in vibrato, arpeggiator, echo, DMC chord loops and a fat mode that spreads one part across all five channels.

Lead idea

Genus

A first-person puzzle game on strange flat surfaces: rooms that wrap around, seams that mirror you, portals that grow or shrink you, and a quail companion who never changes size. A field journal logs each level's species and its surface.

Fat Chip

One fat chiptune instrument for live playing, built from every chip in the room: the C64 is the controller and plays its SID, and the Game Boy and NES join over MIDI.

Fat Chip 64

The controller. Plays the SID and sends MIDI to a Game Boy and a NES, stacking every oscillator into one instrument: poly smart stacking, smart chords and jazz extensions, latch modes, sparkle arpeggios and pads.

Commodore 64 + Kerberos · GitHub

Fat Chip GB

A MIDI sound module for the original Game Boy, in the spirit of mGB. Plays in tandem with Fat Chip 64 or alone from a DAW, with a wavetable wave channel, chord waves, drums and allophone speech.

Game Boy + Teensyboy · GitHub

Music and synths

Sight Reading

Learn to sight-read piano music with a MIDI keyboard: adaptive note, interval, key, chord and melody practice, like a touch-typing course for the staff.

Web app · reevehunter.com/sightreading

Cynthcart Controller

Control Cynthcart on a Commodore 64 over MIDI: every sound parameter, saved patches, for Cynthcart 2.0 or the Kerberos custom build.

Web app · reevehunter.com/C64

Cynthcart Kerberos Build

A personal modification of Paul Slocum's Cynthcart synth for the C64 with a Kerberos MIDI cartridge: redesigned parameter display, per-voice editing, true tremolo, deeper vibrato and more. Not distributed, out of respect for Cynthcart's license.

Commodore 64 · private
