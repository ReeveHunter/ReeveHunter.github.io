PETSCII Screen Bench

Draw a 40 × 25 C64 text screen with its rules: 256 characters from one set, one colour per cell, one background, one border. Paint Fat Chip's screens, then copy the result for Claude.

The C64's own letters and graphics come from its character ROM, which this page doesn't include. Load yours once and the page keeps it: VICE ships it as chargen (in VICE's C64 folder; 4096 bytes). Until then, letters show in a stand-in font and graphics characters as their code.
Move over the screen to see a cell's row, column, character and colour.

Copy for Claude

“Copy screen” puts the whole screen on the clipboard as text: rows of character codes and colours, plus a readable preview. Paste it to Claude in the chat. To load one back, paste it in the box and press “Paste a screen in”.
