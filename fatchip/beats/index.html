Beats

Fat Chip's 25 beats in five banks. Each beat has an A and a B part: play either, both together, or A and B taking turns. Lay a hat pattern or an accent shape over any beat, swing it, or fold it into an odd time signature, on the MidiNES, the Game Boy, or both.

1Beat

A onlyB onlyA and Bhat layerbars: accent layer (strong / normal / soft)

Choosing a beat loads its tempo, swing, hat layer and accent layer; the part and time signature stay. A time signature takes whole runs of the beat's steps from each bar's start (dimmed steps don't play), so the kick stays on the downbeat. Hat layers replace the beat's own closed and open hats; the triplet ones play three to a beat over the straight beat. Accents: hats, cymbals and percussion follow the layer fully, kick and snare only take its strong steps. Keys: Space, A B N M for the part, ← → for the beat.

2Devices

MidiNES

NES rules: one sample at a time (kick, snare, toms, percussion first); hats and cymbals that lose it move to the noise channel. Ride and shaker play on noise. Auto builds a kit for each beat from your Sound Sorter types and tags; your Beat Lab kits are listed too.

Game Boy

Fat Chip GB v0.17's kits, played as the Game Boy plays them: 4-bit at 10.9 kHz, two hits at once (mixed with its soft clip; a third takes over the one nearer its end), volume in its three steps.
