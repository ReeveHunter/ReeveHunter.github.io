Chip Lab

Fat Chip's candidate built-in sounds, played on emulated SID, Game Boy and NES chips by Fat Chip's rules. Rate each one, add notes, and export them for Claude. The emulation is close, not exact.

Devices
On the keys:

Every available chip plays: a sound's important jobs (a sub, an octave, its main tone) move to another chip when you turn one off. Fat Bass adds a bass that stays inside its range (folding by octaves) whatever octave you play in. Like mono / poly, each sound has its own default (tagged "fat bass" when it's on), and you can lock it on or off for every sound, e.g. off for high, airy sets. Play does the same for poly and mono. Effects on a card adjusts that sound's modules; the MIDI tag is how much Game Boy / NES traffic its effects would take. Turn devices off to hear how a sound holds up without them. ▶ plays a demo that suits the sound; click a card to put that sound on your keyboard, MIDI in and the keys at the bottom. Needs marks the devices a sound can't do without, so Fat Chip can offer only the sounds the connected devices can play. Ratings and notes stay in this browser until you export them.

♪Keys:

Click or use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K; Z / X change octave. Chords stack across the chips the way Fat Chip 64 does: as many notes as there are voices, the lowest notes get the leftovers, and Fat Bass (when on) holds the bottom.
