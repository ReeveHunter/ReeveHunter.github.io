Chip Lab

Build Fat Chip patches from the shared effect list, roll the dice, blend two patches, sort the ones you like into Fat Chip's banks, and hear them on the real C64: Chip Lab sends each change to Fat Chip 64 over MIDI (v0.31 plays every setting shown). The older emulated sound library is here.

This browser can't send MIDI. Use Chrome or Edge on the Mac, and allow MIDI (with SysEx) when it asks.

My patches

Click a patch to hear it (it goes to the C64 and opens below). Drag the ones you want in Fat Chip into a bank, under KEEP, GOOD or MAYBE, and drag them about to put them in order; drag one back up to take it out. The C64 has 5 slots a bank (Z X C V B), so KEEP shows how many of the 5 you've used. Dashed tags are the demo patches.

Switch an effect on or off; SID / GB / NES limit it to some chips; 🎲 rolls an effect or a single setting; 🔒 keeps it when you roll; ★ marks the effects that make the patch what it is (its character: kept when voices run short, kept on by the dice). Settings marked · are in the plan but Fat Chip 64 doesn't play them yet, so they're hidden unless you tick Show every setting.

Blend

Moving the slider sends the mixture to the C64 as you go: times and rates mix on a log scale, switches flip half way, an effect only one patch has comes in at the half-way point.

Keys and notes

Click or use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K; Z / X change octave (). The notes go to the C64 on MIDI channel 1, like a keyboard on its MIDI in.
