Chip Lab

The SID, Game Boy and NES emulated together, for prototyping Fat Chip sounds before they go on the real hardware. It's an approximation: close enough to judge ideas, not to replace the chips.

1Play

Tick the voices to play; notes go to them in turn, so ticking several plays chords across chips. Click a voice's name to edit it. Keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play, Z / X change octave.

2Shepard arpeggio

Voices an octave apart play the same arpeggio note. Each one's volume follows the bell below: loud in the middle, silent at the top and bottom, where it wraps around unheard. It sounds like it climbs (or falls) forever. More voices, smoother illusion. Pulses use their duty from section 1; SID voices their waveform.
