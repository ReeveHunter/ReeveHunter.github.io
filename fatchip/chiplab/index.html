Chip Lab

Candidate built-in sounds for Fat Chip, played on emulated SID, Game Boy and NES chips with Fat Chip 64's voice stacking. Rate each one, add notes, and export them for Claude. The emulation is close, not exact.

Devices
On the keys:

Turn devices off to hear how a sound holds up without them. ▶ plays a demo that suits the sound; click a card to put that sound on your keyboard, MIDI in and the keys at the bottom. Needs marks the devices a sound can't do without, so Fat Chip can offer only the sounds the connected devices can play. Ratings and notes stay in this browser until you export them.

♪Keys:

Click or use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K; Z / X change octave. Chords stack across the chips the way Fat Chip 64 does: the lowest notes get the most voices, and the wave and triangle take the bass.
