Sample Lab

Record every MidiNES sample, let the page slice and sort them, then listen and tag.

This browser can't do this page. It needs Web MIDI and audio input: open it in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
Simulation. No MIDI or audio hardware is used: a fake MidiNES makes made-up drum sounds, so the whole page can be tried out. Remove ?sim from the address for the real thing.

1MIDI

Not connected.

Samples are played on the DMC channel (the fifth of the MidiNES's five). The video mode is saved with every clip, since it can change how the NES plays samples.

2Audio in

—

Turn the gain up until the loudest test note peaks around −6 dB without going red. Browser echo cancelling, noise suppression and auto gain are switched off.

3Record

Connect MIDI, choose the audio input and set the video mode to start.

Each note waits for its sound to finish (silence for the time above, or the longest clip), so short drums go quickly and long dialogue gets its full length. Before the run the DMC gets the bank (CC14), the pitch (CC3), and loop, start offset, length, override and Wave Traveller all off. Pitch 12 (CC3 96) is the samples' normal speed (Reeve, by ear on the vocals), so it's the default; the library can play clips back at any other pitch (sped up or slowed down in the browser, which isn't quite the NES's own sound at that pitch). Play through plays the same notes without recording anything, to set levels: with the audio input on it waits for each sound to end and reports the peaks (and the loudest notes at the end); without it, notes are spaced by the play-through spacing.

4Listen and tag

Click a clip or use ↑ ↓ to move, Space to play, 1–5 for the tags (GNARLY, TEXTURE, RHYTHMIC, BASS, KEEP), C to pick a category. Changes are saved in this browser as you go.
