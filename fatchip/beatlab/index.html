Beat Lab

Build MidiNES drum kits by ear: pick a beat, swap sounds while it loops, and let it vary them from your Sound Sorter pairings. Everything plays by NES rules.

1Beat

Click the grid to change the beat (off → hit → accent → ghost); your changes stay with this beat until you reset it. The triangle has no volume control on the NES, so the bass is as loud here as it really is; pulses at 7 are a lead, 5 still on top, 4 a mix.

2Kit

Click a drum to focus it, then ← → try the next sound while the beat plays, ↑ ↓ move between drums, L locks it, M mutes it. Sounds are listed by your types first, then the ones you put in the same bucket as the current one, then the rest. Vary swaps hits for sounds you bucketed together with it (shown green); on noise it nudges pitch and volume. Hats and cymbals can be samples or NES noise.

NES rules: one sample at a time (kick, then snare, tom, percussion, cymbal, open hat, closed hat); a hat or cymbal that loses the sample channel moves to noise, anything else is dropped. One noise sound at a time too, so a closed hat cuts an open hat off.

3Sweep pad

B95–B127 played as one sound. Drag left and right to move through the parts, up and down for the speed; hold still to repeat a part, let go to stop unless Loop is on. Clicking glides from your last click to the new one through every part on the way.
