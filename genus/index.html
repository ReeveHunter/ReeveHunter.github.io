Genus

A prototype. One small level, three kinds of doorway, one quail.

The room you start in has three ways out. One passage loops back on itself. One loops back mirrored. One room has a doorway that leads into itself at a different size.

Field journal

Specimens

This surface

  • North passage: its ends are glued straight across (a cylinder). Arrows point the same way.
  • South passage: its ends are glued with a flip (a Möbius band). Arrows point opposite ways. Go around once and the world is mirrored.
  • West room: its north doorway is glued to its own west doorway, which is half the size. Each trip north halves you; each trip west doubles you.

Your mark on the map is an L. If it reads as a J, you've been mirrored. Press J to close.
